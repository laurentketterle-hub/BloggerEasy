Sunrise Over Angkor Wat

Published: March 15, 2025 • Siem Reap, Cambodia

The ancient stones of Angkor Wat glowed amber in the first light of dawn. Standing before this 12th-century marvel, I felt the weight of centuries pressing against the morning mist. The temple's lotus-shaped towers pierced the sky, their reflections shimmering in the still waters of the moat below.

Local monks in saffron robes moved silently between the galleries, their presence adding a spiritual dimension to the architectural grandeur. The bas-reliefs told stories of epic battles and celestial dancers, each carving a testament to the Khmer Empire's artistic mastery.

Lost in the Streets of Marrakech

Published: February 28, 2025 • Marrakech, Morocco

The medina unfolds like a living maze — narrow alleyways where the scent of spices mingles with the sound of craftsmen hammering copper. Every turn reveals something unexpected: a hidden courtyard garden, a fountain of zellige tiles, a rooftop where storks nest against the Atlas Mountains.

At dusk, Jemaa el-Fnaa square transforms. Smoke rises from food stalls, snake charmers play their flutes, and storytellers draw crowds in circles of lamplight. It's chaos organized by centuries of tradition.

Northern Lights in Tromso

Published: January 10, 2025 • Tromso, Norway

The temperature read minus twenty, but the cold was forgotten the moment the sky ignited. Ribbons of green and violet danced across the Arctic darkness, folding and unfolding like cosmic curtains. The aurora borealis turned a freezing night into the most beautiful show on Earth.

We huddled around a campfire, sipping hot chocolate as the lights reflected in the fjord below. A local Sami guide shared stories of how his ancestors interpreted the lights as the spirits of the departed playing football with a walrus skull.
